Hold a key and speak.

Slash Voice types what you say into the app in front of you. Your speech is turned into text on your own computer and is never uploaded. This is an early build, for testing.

  1. Download and install

    The build is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so your system warns you once. On Windows choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and choose Open Anyway.

  2. Allow the microphone

    On a Mac, also allow accessibility, so the text can be typed into your other apps.

  3. Hold the shortcut and speak

    The first time, the app downloads a speech model. That needs the internet once.
